# Capital Adequacy Policy

## CET1 Requirement

The institution shall maintain a minimum Common Equity Tier 1 (CET1) ratio of **8.5%** at all times, measured on a consolidated basis.

## Internal Buffer

An additional capital conservation buffer of 2.5% applies during stress periods as defined by the Risk Committee.

## Breach Escalation

Any breach of CET1 thresholds must be reported to the Chief Risk Officer within one business day.
